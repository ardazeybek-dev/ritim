How to use it: click a pattern or fill the cells in yourself, then hit PLAY (the space bar works too). Clicking an instrument name mutes that row; the × next to it clears the row.
Making a song: there are four sections — A, B, C, D. Build a different pattern in each, then click the ARRANGEMENT slots to set their order. Switch to SONG mode and it plays the arrangement start to finish. The easiest way in: press one of the demo songs above and take it apart.
Bass line: one note per column. The cells follow the scale, so whichever note you press it stays in key — ROOT changes the pitch, SCALE changes the mood.
DOWNLOAD WAV turns whatever is playing into an audio file on your computer; copy it to your phone and listen to it there.
